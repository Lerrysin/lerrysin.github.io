This tactile sensor embeds an orthogonally woven network of pneumatic channels within a single silicone material. A convolutional neural network decodes the pressure responses to estimate three-dimensional forces and locate contact beyond the physical spacing of the channels.

The study reports an average contact localization error of 2.4 mm and over 98% accuracy in recognizing contact shapes and force orientations.

Published online in *IEEE Sensors Journal* on 14 October 2026 (early access).
